# AIDDLC Standard

AIDDLC (AI-Driven Development Lifecycle) is an open specification that defines how humans and AI systems collaborate to design, build, and deliver software. It establishes clear role boundaries, phase gates, context continuity requirements, and audit obligations — making AI-assisted development both effective and accountable.

The standard is designed for teams where quality, traceability, and regulatory compliance are non-negotiable. It has been proven in production on regulated healthcare and fintech systems. It is equally applicable to any team that wants to move from ad-hoc AI tool use to a systematic, repeatable, and defensible practice.

## The Governing Principle

> **Humans define intent and validate outcomes. AI executes craft and maintains continuity. Every decision is preserved. Every output is auditable. Quality is a system property, not a human heroic act.**

---

## What the Standard Defines

**Two tracks**:
- **Engineering Track** — a seven-phase directed cycle from Foundation through Deploy & Learn, covering the full software development lifecycle with explicit AI and human responsibilities at each phase
- **Product Track** — a four-phase cycle for discovering, prototyping, deploying, and scaling AI-powered product features

**Five governing principles**: Intent over Implementation; Context Never Dies; Gates Before Generation; Continuous Validation; Auditable by Design.

**Five roles**: Intent Authority, Domain Expert, Technical Authority, AI Supervisor, Compliance Officer — each with defined accountability, authority, and AI interaction type.

**Context continuity**: a structured model for preserving all project decisions, artifacts, and rationale across phases, sessions, and personnel changes.

**Compliance mapping**: explicit mapping of regulatory obligations (GDPR, MHRA, CQC, GPhC, FCA, HIPAA, ISO 27001, EU AI Act) to phases and artifacts.

**Two conformance levels**: Full Conformance and Regulated Conformance.
